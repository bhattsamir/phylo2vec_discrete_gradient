Finding the 0.6% of Neurons That Drive Reasoning & Coding via Phylogenetic Trees

When a base language model (Qwen2.5-14B) is fine-tuned into a Coding specialist (Qwen2.5-Coder-14B) or a Math/Reasoning specialist (DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-14B), almost every weight changes slightly due to training noise. How do we isolate the tiny fraction of neurons where true structural rewiring happened? We treat neurons like biological species and build evolutionary trees across every layer.

Step 1 • The 3 Sibling Models

Same Architecture, 3 Skills

We compare three 14B-parameter models that share the exact same 48-layer architecture:

• Base: Qwen2.5-14B (Ancestor)
• Coder: Qwen2.5-Coder-14B
• Reasoner: DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-14B

Step 2 • Neurons as "Species"

48 Trees of 13,824 Neurons

Each layer has 13,824 MLP neurons (gate_proj, up_proj, down_proj). We compute an exact 13,824 × 13,824 cosine distance matrix between neurons on GPU and build a Balanced Minimum Evolution (BME) phylogenetic tree for every layer, partitioning each layer into ~128 evolutionary clades (~108 neurons/clade, 6,171 clades total).

Step 3 • Quartet Violations

Who Changed Partners?

If a group of neurons merely scaled up or rotated together during fine-tuning, their tree relationships (quartets) stay identical. If a clade rewired its functional role, its closest evolutionary neighbors change between Base, Coder, and Reasoner!

Why Does Tree Topology Beat Standard Weight Magnitude (‖ΔW‖F)?

Standard model-merging and pruning methods (like Magnitude, TIES-Merging, or Cosine Drift) ask a 1-body question: "Did neuron i's weight vector move?" During fine-tuning, entire subspaces drift or rotate uniformly without changing the circuit they compute. Phylogenetic Quartet Violation asks a relational N-body question: "Did this clade break its geometric relationship with neighboring neurons in the tree?"

20% Sparse Task-Vector Retention (Keep Only 20% of Fine-Tuned Neurons, Revert 80% to Base)

Higher % = more specialist capability preserved while discarding 80% of fine-tuning updates.

Key Takeaway: Even when we force every method to pick the exact same number of neurons inside every single layer (Per-Layer Stratified 20%), Tree Quartet-Violated Clades still beats flat Spherical k-Means (76.4% vs. 73.3%), TIES-Merging (71.6%), and 3-Matrix Frobenius Norm (74.4%).
