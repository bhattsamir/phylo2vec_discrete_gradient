Manuscript restructuring, 3 November 2026

The canonical source is ../phylo2vec_bme_paper.tex. Its supporting TeX
files contain references, appendices, and reformatted numerical tables.
The root-level duplicate outside paper/ was not changed.

The rewrite uses Introduction, Methods, Experimental design, Results,
Discussion, and Conclusion. The full condensation proof is in Methods
2.3. It specifies component attachment, dyadic normalization, arbitrary
fixed symmetric distances, the within-component constant, and the outside
component. The follow-up revision centres the title, abstract, main methods,
and conclusion on exact BME evaluation and exact condensed distances.
Methods 2.3 now includes an explicit corollary identifying each minibatch
coordinate difference with its full-tree BME difference. Methods 2.4 uses
the same exact condensed matrix for candidate evaluation and acceptance,
and explains why outside distances must reflect preceding commits.
Capped-proposal details, their recorded sequence benchmarks, and regression
counterexamples are in appendices. The sequence timings are explicitly not
measurements of an exact-distance-throughout minibatch search.

Novelty assessment:
Do not claim priority for the underlying fixed-clade BME reduction.
The relevant prior art is Haws, Hodge and Yoshida (2011), Section 2.4.
The manuscript cites it and supplies a self-contained objective-level proof.
The combined coordinate optimizer and parallel implementation may have
distinct contributions, but this targeted search does not establish their
priority or replace a comprehensive algorithmic literature review.

Scientific and numerical corrections:
- Removed a claimed universal cubic optimization runtime. Quadratic sweep
  cost does not specify the number of sweeps.
- Removed the claim that pointer representations prevent GPU execution.
- Separated representational coverage, coordinate-neighborhood coverage,
  SPR-local convergence, and global optimality.
- Removed the unsupported additivity of disjoint update scores.
- Scoped monotonicity to accepted descent updates and the retained best
  tree, not stochastic perturbation trajectories.
- Replaced universal "machine epsilon" claims with observed discrepancies.
- Distinguished skeleton BME changes from its absolute score, which omits C.
- Distinguished bilinear embedding construction from nonlinear sequence
  distances. O(K^2) skeleton evaluation excludes distance-construction cost.
- Used the full-profile/live-outside sequence times already recorded in the
  source draft: 16.84 s and 104.45 s at N=50,000 and N=100,000.
- Corrected initialization-inclusive comparisons by adding the shared
  initialization to BOTH methods: 4.71559x and 2.72517x respectively.
- Explicitly retained the N=10,000 accuracy loss for the minibatch variant.
- Distinguished the in-memory FastME reference from external CLI timings.
- Identified RNASim as simulated data.
- Replaced the interpretation of 2N^2 as a distinct-move count with a
  rectangular workload proxy. Valid coordinates number (N-1)^2.
- Qualified embedding epoch times as batch-count times mean stage times.
